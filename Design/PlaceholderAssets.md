# Temporary development assets

48 runtime textures copied with the user's permission from their existing game:
40 full-body idle/walk/run/throw/death directional atlases and eight enemy frames.
FullBodyArtMetrics.h preserves their registration metadata. They are placeholders
only: the adventurer is not Mara's final appearance and the enemies are not the
final masked wardens/penitents/armor. No original game history or campaign code
was imported. Retained isometric prototype logic derives from local commit 7418cdb.

Do not market these assets as finished An Ember Remains artwork. Replace them
when the new character and enemy designs are approved.
