# First playable floor: The Hushed Cloisters

The supplied Story.md is the narrative source of truth. This document proposes
level structure; it does not change Mara, Elin, the bosses or the ending.

## Route and spaces

- Entry: the hidden cathedral stair. A safe starting chamber.
- Burial chapels: open combat spaces with flanking passages.
- Confiscated relic stores: optional exploration branches.
- Holding cells: narrow approaches and possible future captive interactions.
- Failed healers' infirmary: a story landmark before the final approach.
- Caldris's sanctum: the most distant reachable chamber. Boss placeholder only.

The initial BSP map connects every chamber, with wide corridors, persistent
exploration and no room-clear loading transitions. Room roles and the farthest
sanctum are deterministic from the seed. The minimap exposes explored tiles only.

## Visual direction (not final artwork)

Ash-dark limestone, worn ivory burial floors, oxidized bronze fixtures, muted
red wax and a small warm ember against cold darkness. Cathedral geometry should
feel inhabited and repurposed for detention, not randomly stacked boxes.
Later passes need modular floors, wall corners, arches, cell bars, relic shelves,
infirmary props, memory traces and obscuring-wall transparency.

## Combat direction

Magic, not swords. First input prototype: aim a cinder projectile with the mouse
and hold LMB to cast. It stops at walls and hits a target on impact. This is a
mechanical placeholder, not approved Mara casting art or finished spell VFX.
Future decisions: casting resources, spell loadout, residue harvesting and memory
progression. Do not import the old game's tea, FREEDOM, weapon loot or bosses.

## Milestones

1. Validate continuous navigation, camera, room roles and readable spaces.
2. Replace placeholder character/enemies with Mara and Cloister inhabitants.
3. Build cinderbinding combat and residue progression.
4. Add Caldris, the records reveal and descent into the Glassworks.

Only the first floor blockout and basic spell-input prototype are implemented.
Narrative interactions, final props, bosses and the other four regions are plans.
