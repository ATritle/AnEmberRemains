Adaptations include mono conversion, trimming, gain normalization, playback
rate changes, selected fragments, reversal, and short room echoes.
The footsteps combine the RPG pack's small wooden impact with authored
stone transients. Dungeon wind, water-drop resonators, candle/fire textures,
and electrical buzz layers were synthesized specifically for this project.

Enemy combat feedback reuses these assets with per-enemy pitch and gain: cloth, metal-like shatter, magical ignition, phase and arcane cues. No additional third-party audio sources were added.
